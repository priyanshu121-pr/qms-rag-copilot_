SOP-WLD-001: Welding Inspection Procedure
Purpose: Ensure all welds meet quality standard ISO 5817 level B.
Scope: Applies to all MIG welding stations on Line 2.
Procedure:
1. Operator performs visual inspection of every weld within 5 minutes of completion.
2. Check for cracks, porosity, undercut and incomplete fusion.
3. Any weld with crack or incomplete fusion must be rejected immediately and tagged red.
4. If more than 3 rejects occur in one batch of 100, stop the line and inform the Quality Engineer.
5. Record all findings in the Inspection Log form QF-12.
6. Shielding gas flow must be verified at 15-18 L/min at the start of every shift.
