SOP-CAL-003: Measuring Instrument Calibration Procedure
Purpose: Ensure all inspection and measuring instruments give accurate readings.
Scope: Vernier calipers, micrometers, torque wrenches, pressure gauges, CMM.
Calibration frequency:
- Vernier calipers and micrometers: every 6 months.
- Torque wrenches: every 3 months or after 5000 cycles, whichever comes first.
- Pressure gauges: every 12 months.
Procedure:
1. Metrology lab calibrates against traceable national standards.
2. A calibration sticker with due date is attached to every instrument.
3. Instruments past due date must be removed from use and tagged "DO NOT USE".
4. If an instrument is found out of tolerance, all products inspected since the last valid calibration must be reviewed by the Quality Engineer.
