Supplier Audit Report SA-2026-Q2: Precision Castings Pvt Ltd
Audit date: 18 May 2026
Auditor: Quality Assurance Team
Scope: Aluminium housing castings for Gearbox GX-90.
Findings:
1. Major: Incoming material certificates were missing for 2 of 10 sampled lots.
2. Minor: Dimensional inspection records not signed by the inspector.
3. Observation: Furnace temperature logging is manual; automated logging recommended.
Result: Conditional approval. Supplier must submit corrective action plan within 30 days.
Supplier rating after audit: B (previously A).
